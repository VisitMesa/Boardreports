National Travel Context
Source: Tourism Economics · U.S. Travel Association · MMGY Global Portrait of American Travelers
  • Airline fares up 37.9% vs. prior year as of May — the sharpest input cost increase across all travel categories. F&B up 31.9%, transportation up 24.0%. These macro headwinds have not dampened Mesa demand, which outperformed the comp set.
  • Travel spending is +8.3% vs. 2019 nationally (Tourism Economics), continuing a multiyear recovery trajectory.
  • Oxford Economics upgraded its 2026 GDP forecast to +2.2% (from +1.9%) and consumer spending to +1.9%. Tourism Economics raised its U.S. RevPAR forecast to +2.8% YOY. Positive tailwinds for the summer and fall booking window.
  • High-income travelers remain most committed: 91–93% of households earning $150K+ plan leisure travel in the next 12 months (2026 Q1 MMGY data), reinforcing Mesa's opportunity with its premium visitor segment.
  • Summer booking pace is strong: As of June 10, June hotel reservations are up 4.4% YOY, July up 5.0%, and September up 9.2% — all ahead of last year at the same point in the booking window.

GEO/AI Visibility is growing rapidly: VisitMesa.com received 334 AI-referred sessions in May (+43.3% MOM), with ChatGPT alone driving 311 sessions. Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity account for the remaining sessions. 84,858 pages now appear in Google AI Overviews (+46.4% YOY) — meaning Visit Mesa content is increasingly surfaced when travelers use AI tools to research Mesa. Top AI-referred pages include /events/ (35 sessions), homepage (15), /blog/top-things-to-do-in-mesa (15), and /events/fifa-2026 (14).

The decline in traditional organic metrics is a sector-wide shift, not a Visit Mesa-specific issue. As AI tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity answer travel questions directly — "what are the best things to do in Mesa?" — users increasingly get their answers without clicking through to websites at all. This "zero-click" behavior is compressing organic click volume across destination marketing organizations nationally. The fact that URL clicks are down (-26.3% YOY) while impressions are only down (-28.3%) and average position is improving (+2.6% MOM) indicates the site is still ranking well — travelers are simply engaging differently. Visit Mesa is ahead of many peers in adapting: 84,858 pages now surface in AI Overviews (+46.4% YOY), meaning the brand is present in the new search experience even when users don't click. May deliverables — metadata and content updates to landing pages and blog pages — directly target AI-overview eligibility and GEO performance. This investment positions Visit Mesa to capture intent in both traditional and AI-driven search environments as the landscape continues to evolve.

Key Metric Definitions
TermDefinition
ADR (Average Daily Rate)Average revenue earned per occupied hotel room per night.
RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)Total room revenue divided by total available rooms. Combines both occupancy and rate into one efficiency metric.
Occupancy RatePercentage of available hotel rooms that were sold on a given night or period.
ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)Revenue generated for every $1 spent on advertising. A ROAS of 40x means $40 in bookings for every $1 in ad spend.
Room NightsThe total number of hotel rooms occupied multiplied by the number of nights. One guest staying 3 nights = 3 room nights.
Overnight Trip ShareThe percentage of total visitor trips that included at least one overnight stay. Measured among visitors from 50+ miles.
Day TripA visit where the traveler spends at least 5 hours in the market but returns home the same day. Measured among visitors from 50+ miles away.
Overnight LeakageIdentified when a day trip to Mesa is preceded or followed by an overnight stay in a different market. Those overnight stays = Nights Lost: visitors Mesa attracts but does not capture in lodging.
Search-to-Booking RateThe percentage of VisitMesa.com visitors who go on to complete a travel booking. Measured via ADARA. Indicates how effectively the website converts research intent into confirmed trips.
CTR (Click-Through Rate)Percentage of ad impressions that resulted in a click.
Engagement Rate (Social)Interactions (likes, comments, shares, saves) as a percentage of total audience or impressions, depending on platform.
Engagement Rate (Website)In GA4, the percentage of sessions that lasted longer than 10 seconds, had a conversion event, or had 2+ page views. The inverse of bounce rate.
FYTDFiscal Year-to-Date. Visit Mesa's fiscal year runs July 1 – June 30. FYTD May 2026 = July 1, 2025 through May 31, 2026.
VCR (Video Completion Rate)Percentage of video ad impressions where the viewer watched the video to completion.
