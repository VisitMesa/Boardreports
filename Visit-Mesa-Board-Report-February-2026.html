Practical AI Webinar Series for Business Partners

Visit Mesa launched a three-part Practical AI Webinar Series for local business partners in February, hosted by Greg Oates, Founder of 2050 City and advisor to 100+ tourism organizations worldwide. Sessions covered AI for customer service and brand communications (Feb. 10), keeping human connection at the center of AI-assisted workflows (Feb. 10), and how AI is reshaping search and online discovery (Feb. 19) — the latter presented by Dylan Scacchetti, MarTech Director at Madden Media. The watch-and-learn format required no technical background and was open to all Mesa business partners. Promotional posts drove strong engagement and were reshared widely across the Visit Mesa network.

Key Metric Definitions
TermDefinition
ADR (Average Daily Rate)Average revenue earned per occupied hotel room per night.
RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)Total room revenue divided by total available rooms. Combines both occupancy and rate into one efficiency metric.
Occupancy RatePercentage of available hotel rooms that were sold on a given night or period.
ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)Revenue generated for every $1 spent on advertising. A ROAS of 40x means $40 in bookings for every $1 in ad spend.
Room NightsThe total number of hotel rooms occupied multiplied by the number of nights. One guest staying 3 nights = 3 room nights.
Overnight Trip ShareThe percentage of total visitor trips that included at least one overnight stay (vs. day trips that return home same day). Measured among visitors from 50+ miles.
Day TripA visit where the traveler spends at least 5 hours in the market but returns home the same day without an overnight stay. Measured among visitors from 50+ miles away.
Overnight LeakageIdentified when a day trip to Mesa is preceded or followed by an overnight stay in a different market — one that is neither Mesa nor the visitor's home location. Those overnight stays are counted as Nights Lost: visitors who were in market but chose (or defaulted) to sleep elsewhere. This represents demand Mesa attracts but does not capture in its own lodging inventory.
Search-to-Booking RateMeasured via ADARA. The percentage of VisitMesa.com visitors who go on to complete a travel booking (hotel, flight, or package). Indicates how effectively the website converts research intent into confirmed trips.
CTR (Click-Through Rate)Percentage of ad impressions that resulted in a click.
Engagement Rate (Social)Interactions (likes, comments, shares, saves) as a percentage of total audience or impressions, depending on platform.
Engagement Rate (Website)In GA4, the percentage of sessions that lasted longer than 10 seconds, had a conversion event, or had 2+ page views. The inverse of bounce rate.
